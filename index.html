Install in 30 seconds

No Python, no admin rights, nothing else to install.

Download

Click Download for Windows to get LaptopBuddySetup.exe.

Open it

Double-click the file. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway.

Click Install

Your buddy pops up right away and lives in your Start menu. It can start with Windows too.

Why the Windows warning? Laptop Buddy is a new, free app that isn't code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen doesn't recognise it. It doesn't need admin rights and installs only for your user account.

Questions

Is it free?

Yes, completely free.

Does it send my data anywhere?

No. Everything runs on your computer and works offline. Your stats and pet names are saved only on your laptop. The mic only listens after you press the Mic button, using Windows' built-in speech recognition.

How do I use it?

Click your pet for today's stats and buttons (water, sit, feed, play, chat, rename, switch pets). Drag it anywhere. Right-click for the full menu.

How do I close or uninstall it?

Right-click the pet → Quit buddy. To uninstall, open Settings → Apps → Installed apps, find Laptop Buddy and click Uninstall.

Does it work on Mac?

Not yet – Windows 10 and 11 only.
